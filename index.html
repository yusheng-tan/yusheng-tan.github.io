Research Assistant, University of Chicago

I am a Research Assistant at the University of Chicago, working with Prof. Guihong Wan on language-model agents that turn wearable data into personal health guidance. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Washington University in St. Louis and my B.Eng. from North China Electric Power University. Previously, I worked with Prof. Xiaoxiao Li at UBC on pathology foundation models.
